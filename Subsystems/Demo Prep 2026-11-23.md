# Demo Prep 2026-11-23

Evening of 23 Nov 2026, the night before the presentation. Goal: a live demo that shows what the
system sees (face mesh, eyes, phone, drinking, watch heart rate, the alert) on one screen, working
on the real Pi. Work was driven by live recordings on the Pi, not by guesses.

> [!summary] State at the pause
> - **Live dashboard** runs on the Pi as a boot-start service: `http://<pi>:8000`.

>   decision, and fps / processing ms / watch round trip / CPU temp / load / power. Calibrate and
>   Reset buttons.
> - Distraction now follows the report: **gaze or head off the road > 2 s** (face model), plus the

> - Everything is on branches, merged together in the pre-release branch `demo/pre-2026-11-23`;

Tried and removed the same evening: an "eyes hidden" gate that stopped drowsiness while the phone
classifier saw a hand / phone / cup at the face. The classifier says call / texting while the
driver just sits, so it hid the eyes in **66 %** of frames and stalled calibration ("waiting for
your face" for a minute). The face model's own gate (eye channel invalid past 30 deg yaw / 25 deg
pitch from the calibrated pose) already does this job.

## Decisions (D29-D35)

Added to [[Project Log 2026-11#Decisions]].

- **D29** Live demo dashboard is a separate `demo/dashboard/` server on the Pi; it reads what the Pi already serves and changes neither models nor runtime.
- **D30** The runtime-owned camera draws the face mesh (`--mesh`); the Pi bundle's own `pipeline.yaml` picks the face backend.
- **D31** Face crop from the full-resolution frame on the Pi (`facemesh.hires_crop`).
- **D32** No phone-classifier gate on drowsiness; the face model's head-angle eye gate is the only one.
- **D33** Texting removed; head-down is face-model distraction (report: off-road > 2 s). Eating and texting are never reported by the v2 classifier.
- **D34** Eyes-open reference frozen at calibration; no drowsiness verdict until the eyes reopen after a calibration.
- **D35** Drinking on the dashboard is a 1.5 s average of the drinking score (>= 0.4); still display only (D28).

## Report vs implementation (audit)

| Report | Implementation | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Flag gaze off the road > 2 s | Head > 25 deg yaw / > 20 deg pitch or iris gaze off, for 2 s, relative to the calibrated pose; fusion -> short beep | Matches; iris part noisy at this face size (open) |
| Alert on eyes closed beyond 2-3 s | Microsleep at **1.5 s** (code comment: 2 s at 100 km/h is 55 m blind) | Stricter than the report: decide code or wording |
| PERCLOS + blink rate | P80, 60 s window, warning 15 %, drowsy 25 %, >= 60 % valid frames; blinks per minute | Matches (report gives no numbers) |
| Graded alerts | distracted -> short beep, drowsy -> repeated, phone -> continuous, unresponsive -> max | Matches; "combined with HR" max is not live (HR in shadow) |
| Calibrate for position, posture, lighting | 10 s open + 3 s closed (+2 s settle each): EAR open/closed, neutral head pose, gaze centre, quality grade, rejected below 0.04 separation | Position + posture yes; lighting only via the IR camera |

Nothing in the app has to change for today's work: the phone link contract is unchanged, the app
already knows the `starting` state that now follows a calibration, `texting` simply stays false,
and drinking is not shown in the app (D28). Optional clean-ups (not done; another session has
uncommitted app work in `RTDMS-reset`):

- Diagnostics shows a "Texting" row that can no longer fire (`screens/diagnostics/raw-metrics.tsx`).
- Diagnostics labels openness "Eye openness (EAR)"; it is calibrated openness, not EAR (`i18n/diagnostics.ts`).
- Demo mode still plays a "texting" phase (`demo/scenario.ts`); do not use demo mode to show texting.

**Calibration in the app** (`app/calibrate.tsx`, onboarding `eye-calibration.tsx`) follows the
unit's own steps (`calibration_step`, seconds left) and needs no change to work. Two wording
changes follow from tonight (not done; `app/src/i18n/en.ts` is also edited by the uncommitted
`RTDMS-reset` work):

- Step 1 says "Look ahead with your eyes open". People then open their eyes **wide**, and since
  the eyes-open reference is now frozen at calibration (D34), relaxed eyes would read below 100 %.
  Better: "Look ahead and blink normally (don't open your eyes wide)".
- The reference no longer adapts, so a calibration is only valid for that seat and camera
  position: add "Calibrate again if the unit or the seat moves" to the intro or the done screen.
- After "Calibration complete" the unit reports `starting` for up to 5 s until the eyes are seen
  open (D34); the app already shows that state as "Starting".

## Pi operations learned

- `pi/deploy/deploy.sh` does not work from Git Bash on Windows: `find` resolves to Windows `find`, so it reports "0 file(s) differ". Files were sent with `git show <ref>:<path> | ssh ... 'cat > ...'`, a backup in `~/rtdms-pi/.bak-<stamp>/code.tgz` first, then checked by md5.
- Tailscale drops mid-deploy happen: send all files in **one** ssh call (tar) and check every md5 afterwards.
- A reboot needs the Pi's sudo password (not stored anywhere by design); services restart without it: `systemctl --user restart rtdms-pi-user rtdms-dashboard`.
- The Pi rebooted by itself 5 times on 23 Nov (05:46, 06:39, 06:48, 07:34, 07:46 Pi time); at least two were from moving it. Logs end abruptly (power cut or hard freeze; the systemd hardware watchdog is 1 min). Power read 5.13-5.15 V and `throttled=0x0` afterwards.
- `/tmp` is cleared at every reboot (the recorder has to be copied again).

## Open at the pause

- **Looking away** while looking ahead: measure with the new `d_yaw` / `d_pitch` / `gaze_dev` fields (1-minute test: ahead, left, right, down, eyes only), then fix the iris branch (likely horizontal only or a higher threshold, or head pose only).
- **Hand / phone at the face read as drinking**: proposed "call wins" (frames whose top class is call count 0 toward the drinking average).
- **Camera placement**: side camera (report: beside the right door) = good drinking / phone, poor eyes; close frontal = good eyes, weaker drinking. Demo compromise: 50-80 cm, 25-30 deg to the side, then recalibrate.
- **Unexplained reboots**: official 27 W supply, short cable fixed in place.
- **Merging**: PR #94 and the branches above are not on `main`.
- **Overlap**: the uncommitted work in `RTDMS-reset` (`pi/perclos-reset-after-calibration`) adds a `/reset` command and its own post-calibration grace in `drowsiness.py` / `stream.py`; it will conflict with `distraction/occlusion-gate`. The dashboard Reset button waits for that `/reset`.
