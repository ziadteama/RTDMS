# 4.5 Initial Cost Calculation and BOM Analysis

↑ [[04 Experiment and Results]]

## 4.5.1 BOM Estimate and Hardware Recommendation

The Bill of Materials (BOM) estimate compares the main hardware options required for the proposed DMS prototype. The analysis focuses on edge computing, AI acceleration, camera sensing, and wearable vital-sign sensing, because these components directly affect system feasibility, cost, and real-time performance.

### 4.5.1.1 Edge Compute Options

#### Table 4-4: Edge Compute Options and Public Prices

| Product | Specification and Prototype Fit | Public Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB) | Quad-core Arm Cortex-A76 at 2.4 GHz. Recommended baseline compute board for camera pipeline, sensor interface, logging, and control logic. | 13,000 EGP |
| Raspberry Pi AI HAT+ (13 TOPS) | Hailo-8L accelerator; 13 TOPS; PCIe interface. Optional lower-cost acceleration option. | 8,000 EGP |
| Raspberry Pi AI HAT+ (26 TOPS) | Hailo-8 accelerator; 26 TOPS; PCIe interface. Recommended for multi-model computer-vision pipelines. | 8,750 EGP |
| Raspberry Pi AI HAT+ 2 | Hailo-10H accelerator; 40 TOPS INT4; 8GB on-board RAM. Upgrade option for future GenAI or VLM features. | 12,000 EGP |
| NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super | High-performance edge AI board; up to 67 TOPS; 8GB LPDDR5. Justified only if Raspberry Pi 5 cannot meet latency targets. | 28,000 EGP |

For a realistic student MVP, Raspberry Pi 5 remains the most suitable starting point. For driver-monitoring computer vision, the AI HAT+ 26 TOPS is the recommended accelerator because it is directly aligned with real-time CV workloads.

### 4.5.1.2 Camera Options

#### Table 4-5: Camera Options and Public Prices

| Product | Key Specification | Public Price | Strengths | Limits / Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pi IR camera 5MP 130-degree | Night-vision / IR-ready | 750 EGP | Very low cost; useful for dark cabin tests | Narrower coverage and weaker image quality |
| Pi IR camera 5MP 160-degree | Night-vision / wider field of view | 1,050 EGP | Recommended first-build; wider cabin coverage at low cost | Wide-angle distortion may require calibration |
| Pi Camera Module 3 / NoIR | 12MP Sony IMX708 autofocus | 2,250–4,500 EGP | Better image quality and official ecosystem support | Visible-light version insufficient for night driving |
| Pi AI Camera | Sony IMX500 intelligent sensor | 5,650 EGP | On-sensor AI processing | Higher cost and less flexible |
| Pi HQ camera + IR lens | 12.3MP modular optics path | 5,000–5,700 EGP | Better optics and lens flexibility | Higher cost for MVP |

The Pi IR camera 5MP 160-degree module is recommended for the first build. It provides wider cabin coverage than the 130-degree option while remaining affordable.

### 4.5.1.3 Vital-Sign Sensor Options (MAX Wristband Sensors)

#### Table 4-6: MAX Wristband Sensor Options

| MAX Sensor | Vital-Sign Signal | Integration Type | Key Strengths | Limits / Design Notes | Project Fit | Public Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MAX30102 | HR and SpO2 PPG | I2C module | Low cost, compact, widely documented | Motion artifacts must be filtered; not medical-grade | Recommended first-build sensor | 95–520 EGP |
| MAX30101 | HR and SpO2 PPG | Chip/module | Improved LED/sensor capability | May require careful sourcing or PCB integration | Technical upgrade option | Varies |
| MAXM86161 | PPG optical sensor | Module/evaluation | Higher-quality optical sensing | More complex and expensive | Advanced upgrade | Varies |
| MAX86140/86141 | Optical AFE for PPG | Analog front-end | High-performance signal acquisition | Requires external optical design and PCB | Research-grade option | Varies |
| MAX86150 | PPG + ECG path | Integrated sensor | Combined optical and electrical sensing | More complex wearable design | Future multimodal upgrade | Varies |

The MAX30102 wristband module is recommended for the final BOM because it has public module prices, is easy to prototype, and is sufficient for HR/HRV-based fatigue and stress-support analysis.

> **Total: 22,895–23,320 EGP** (MAX30102 price range dependent). Re-check prices immediately before purchasing — local electronics-market prices move quickly.
