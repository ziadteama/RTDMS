## Abstract

Road accidents caused by driver drowsiness, distraction, and inattention remain a critical global safety issue, creating a strong need for intelligent and reliable Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS) that can operate in real time under diverse driving conditions. This project addresses the gap between academic research and commercial DMS solutions by proposing a cost-effective, technically feasible system capable of detecting driver drowsiness, gaze deviation, and phone usage. The proposed system adopts a vision-based architecture incorporating a camera module, real-time driver-state detection algorithms (eye closure, head pose, and gaze tracking), an embedded processing unit, and an alert mechanism for timely warnings. Findings indicate that a camera-based DMS offers an effective balance between performance and affordability while remaining scalable for integration into a V2C Smart Dash Camera. Overall, this work provides a structured and implementation-ready foundation for enhancing road safety through intelligent driver monitoring and future AI-driven system expansion.
